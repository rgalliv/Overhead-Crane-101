# 📕 OHC — OSHA Part 1910 Map

**&#167;1910.179 does not stand alone.** It routes to other parts of 1910 by name, and it makes consensus standards enforceable through &#167;1910.6. The ACS cites &#167;1910.179 and stops. ⬅ [[OHC — 1910.179 Citation Pack (verified)]] · [[_Crane Standards — Index]]

> [!important] The symmetry that was missed
> The cross-check already found that **OHC-05 and OHC-06 have no construction-branch inspection authority** &#8212; &#167;1926.1412 and &#167;1926.1413 appear nowhere in the ACS.
> The **same hole exists on the facility branch**, and for the same reason: the ACS reaches for the consensus standard where a regulation exists. OHC-06 cites **ASME B30.9 by name only**. On the facility branch the governing rule is **29 CFR 1910.184**, which is law, not guidance.

### ⭐ The two-edition trap

Two different regulations incorporate two different vintages of the same standard family, on two different branches:

| Branch | Regulation | Incorporates | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Facility** | &#167;1910.179(b)(2) via &#167;1910.6 | **ANSI B30.2.0&#8209;1967** | cranes constructed and installed **on or after 31 Aug 1971** |
| **Construction** | &#167;1926.1438(b)(2)(ii) via &#167;1926.6 | **ASME B30.2&#8209;2005** sections | equipment manufactured **on or after 19 Sep 2001** |

An operator who learns "B30.2 is the overhead crane standard" and stops there will cite the wrong edition on one of the two branches, roughly half the time. **This is a teachable distinction and the ACS does not currently carry it.**

It also reframes the &#167;3 discipline. Where a consensus standard is **incorporated by reference**, its named sections are **regulation**. "Cite by name only" is the right rule for consensus guidance and the wrong rule for text OSHA has adopted.

## 3. Two branches, two regulatory spines &#8212; the corrected picture

| Topic | Facility branch | Construction branch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crane operating rules | &#167;1910.179 (all) | &#167;1910.179 (enumerated paragraphs only) |
| **Inspection** | &#167;1910.179(j) | **&#167;1926.1412** |
| **Wire rope** | &#167;1910.179(m) | **&#167;1926.1413** |
| **Slings / rigging** | **&#167;1910.184** | **&#167;1926.251** |
| **Energy control** | **&#167;1910.147** | **&#167;1910.147** (substituted at &#167;1926.1438(b)(2)(ii)) |
| Ladders, walkways | **Subpart D** | &#167;1926 Subpart X |
| Electrical | **Subpart S** | &#167;1926 Subpart K |
| Operator requirement | &#167;1910.179(b)(8) designated personnel | &#167;1926.1427 certification |

**Only &#167;1910.147 is common to both.** Everything else forks. An ACS that teaches one column and calls it "OSHA" is teaching half the standard of care.

| **Subpart D** / **Subpart S** | ❌ no primary text |
| **&#167;1910.6** | ❌ no primary text |
| &#167;1926 Subpart CC | ⚠️ held, degraded OCR at &#167;1926.1438 |

> [!warning] The discipline this triggers
> CM-101's rule is *"the module cites no document the team has not read."* Right now the ACS could cite &#167;1910.184 and &#167;1910.147 only from **secondary material** &#8212; decks and client policies that paraphrase them.
> **Acquire the primary text before authoring against these sections.** Part 1910 is public domain and small; this is a cheap gap to close, unlike the P-307 or Crane Corner pulls.

## 5. What this changes

**New elements needed**
- **OHC-01 or OHC-02** &#8212; the **&#167;1910.6 incorporation-by-reference mechanism** and the **two-edition trap** (ANSI B30.2.0-1967 on the facility branch, ASME B30.2-2005 sections on the construction branch)
- **OHC-06 Task A** &#8212; **&#167;1910.184** as the facility-branch sling regulation, paired with &#167;1926.251 on the construction branch
- **OHC-05 Task C** &#8212; **&#167;1910.147** named explicitly as the energy-control standard behind tag-out

**Reference blocks to amend**
- **OHC-02** add Subpart S and Subpart D
- **OHC-03** add Subpart D (cab access, the 12-inch gap rule)
- **OHC-05** add &#167;1910.147 and &#167;1926.1412
- **OHC-06** add &#167;1910.184 and &#167;1926.1413 / &#167;1926.251
- **OHC-10** add &#167;1910.147, &#167;1910.176, Subpart S

**Standing-discipline amendment**
&#167;3 says consensus standards are cited *by name only*. That is correct for consensus guidance and **wrong for text incorporated by reference**. Where &#167;1910.6 or &#167;1926.6 has adopted named sections, those sections are regulation and should be cited as such.
