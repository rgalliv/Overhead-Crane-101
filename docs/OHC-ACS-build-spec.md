### OHC-01 Equipment Types, Configurations, and Jurisdictional Framework

**Objective.** Determine the equipment class, installation status, and governing regulatory regime for any overhead or gantry crane before operation.

**References:** 29 CFR 1910.179 · 29 CFR §1926.1438 · §1910.6 IBR · EM 385-1-1 (15 Mar 2024) para 16-2, 16-8.aa · ANSI B30.2.0-1967 (facility IBR) · ASME B30.2-2005 sections (construction IBR) · ASME B30.2-2016 (vault consensus copy, not IBR) · ASME B30.17 (by name only)

#### Task A — Equipment Identification and Classification7K3R3S

**Objective.** Identify overhead and gantry crane types and configurations and their operational characteristics.

**Knowledge**

OHC.01.A.K1 Equipment family under §1926.1438(b)(1): overhead/bridge, gantry, semigantry, cantilever gantry, wall, storage bridge, and launching gantry cranes, irrespective of travel means

OHC.01.A.K2 Top-running vs. underhung bridge and trolley configurations and their structural implications

OHC.01.A.K3 Single-girder vs. double-girder bridge construction and capacity/service implications

OHC.01.A.K4 Monorail and underhung systems and wall-mounted jib cranes as related equipment classes

OHC.01.A.K5 Governing consensus standard by configuration: top-running (ASME B30.2) vs. underhung trolley or bridge (ASME B30.17)

OHC.01.A.K6 Hoist as a distinct component class (ASME B30.16)

OHC.01.A.K7 Incorporation by reference (§1910.6): a consensus standard named is guidance; a standard incorporated by reference is enforceable as regulation for the sections named

**Risk Management**

OHC.01.A.R1 Misclassifying equipment and applying the wrong inspection, marking, or operating rule set

OHC.01.A.R2 Assuming mobile-crane operating logic (outriggers, load charts by radius) transfers to fixed-path equipment

OHC.01.A.R3 Operating unfamiliar configurations without configuration-specific familiarization

**Skills**

OHC.01.A.S1 Identify crane type, girder configuration, and trolley arrangement on sight

OHC.01.A.S2 Match a given crane to its governing ASME volume by name

OHC.01.A.S3 Locate and interpret the equipment identification and capacity markings

#### Task B — Jurisdictional Determination7K3R3S

**Objective.** Determine which federal regime governs a specific crane based on installation status and work context.

**Knowledge**

OHC.01.B.K1 Permanently installed cranes used in construction: §1926.1438(a) applies 29 CFR 1910.179 (except §1910.179(b)(1)), not Subpart CC

OHC.01.B.K2 Not permanently installed, used in construction: §1926.1438(b) applies designated Subpart CC sections including §1926.1427 operator certification, plus specified §1910.179 paragraphs

OHC.01.B.K3 Specified §1910.179 paragraphs under the §1926.1438(b) hybrid: (b)(5)-(7); (e)(1),(3),(5),(6); (f)(1),(4); (g); (h)(1),(3); (k); (n); and (a) definitions except hoist and load

OHC.01.B.K4 General industry facility operation outside construction: 29 CFR 1910.179 as the direct spine

OHC.01.B.K5 Federal/USACE work: EM 385-1-1 Chapter 16 supplemental requirements and Class I / Class II crane-hoist tiers (para 16-2.g/h)

OHC.01.B.K6 Indicators of permanent installation: physically fastened to the building, not readily assembled or disassembled, irremovable part of the property

OHC.01.B.K7 Two IBR editions, two cut-offs: facility branch incorporates ANSI B30.2.0-1967 for cranes constructed and installed on or after 31 Aug 1971 (§1910.179(b)(2)); construction branch incorporates named ASME B30.2-2005 sections for equipment manufactured on or after 19 Sep 2001 (§1926.1438(b)(2)(iii)). The 2016 volume is not either governing edition

**Risk Management**

OHC.01.B.R1 Applying facility (general industry) rules to a non-permanently installed crane in construction, missing the §1926.1427 certification requirement on that branch

OHC.01.B.R2 Assuming Subpart CC applies to a permanently installed crane used for a construction task

OHC.01.B.R3 Failing to re-evaluate jurisdiction when a crane or work context changes

**Skills**

OHC.01.B.S1 Walk the installation-status decision tree for a given crane and state the governing regime

OHC.01.B.S2 Identify which certification or designation requirement attaches on each branch

OHC.01.B.S3 Classify a given crane as EM 385 Class I or Class II from control mode and capacity

#### Task C — Roles, Qualification Architecture, and Standard of Care5K3R3S

**Objective.** Explain who qualifies the operator, what the designation covers, and the operator's standard of care.

**Knowledge**

OHC.01.C.K1 Employer as the qualified person making the operator qualification determination; training establishes the operator's standard of care and does not itself confer qualification

OHC.01.C.K2 Designation Gate model on the facility branch: employer-issued designation, documented

OHC.01.C.K3 Certification Gate on the §1926.1438(b) construction branch: §1926.1427 certified/licensed operator (accredited crane operator testing organization or qualifying government license). NCCCO publishes a CCO Overhead Crane Operator certification; programme routing through CCOS is administrative, not a CFR name

OHC.01.C.K4 Competent Person (Cranes and Rigging) role under EM 385 para 16-2.i on federal work

OHC.01.C.K5 Distinction between operator designation, inspection personnel, and maintenance qualified persons

**Risk Management**

OHC.01.C.R1 Treating course completion as qualification without the employer determination

OHC.01.C.R2 Operating on federal work without the required written designation

OHC.01.C.R3 Blurred accountability when roles are undocumented

**Skills**

OHC.01.C.S3 Refuse assignments outside the scope of designation

**Skills**

OHC.02.B.S2 Verify brake holding on a test lift before full hoisting

**Skills**
