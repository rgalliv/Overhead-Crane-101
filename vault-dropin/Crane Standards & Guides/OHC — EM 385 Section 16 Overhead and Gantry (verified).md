## Three things this changes in the ACS

**1. A new element is needed — the wind alarm.**
The wind-indicating alarm requirement has no element. It is distinct from `OHC.08.C.K2` (secure when leaving) and from `OHC.10.B.K1` (wind effects generally): this is a **specific alarm, a specific trigger, and a mandatory stop**. Add to **OHC-10 Task B** or **OHC-08 Task C**. Note it is scoped to **cab-operated outdoor cranes**.

**2. EM 385 Section 16 does not split by installation status.**
It applies *"whether permanently installed in a facility or not."* That is the **opposite** of the OSHA structure, where §1926.1438 splits the world on exactly that question. On federal work the supplemental requirements attach to **both** branches.

This is a genuinely teachable distinction and the ACS currently flattens it: `OHC.01.B.K5` treats EM 385 as a third branch alongside the two OSHA branches, when it is better understood as **a layer that sits across both**. Worth rewording.

**3. Precision on the marking rule.**
The rated load is *"plainly marked on each side of the crane"*; the **legible-from-ground-or-floor** requirement attaches specifically to markings on the **bridge, trolley and load block**. §1910.179(b)(5) phrases it differently — there the legibility clause attaches to the multi-hoist marking. Minor, but `OHC.04.A.K1` currently blends the two sources into one sentence and should cite them separately.
