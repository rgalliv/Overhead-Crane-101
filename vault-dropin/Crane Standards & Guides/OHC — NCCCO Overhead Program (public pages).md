## What the public overview actually publishes

From the Certification Overview page (paraphrase with attribution, not a dump of exam content):

- NCCCO publishes a **CCO Overhead Crane Operator** certification program.
- Eligibility includes: at least 18 years of age; comply with NCCCO Substance Abuse Policy and Code of Ethics; **pass Written Examination**; **pass Practical Examination**; both corresponding exams within a **12-month** timeframe.
- Written exam: a **single** Overhead Crane operation examination, **65 multiple-choice** questions, **60 minutes**.
- Practical exam available on **Cab Operated** or **Pendant/Remote Control** overhead crane types.
- Certification period: **five years**. Recertification requirements must be completed during the 12 months prior to expiration.
