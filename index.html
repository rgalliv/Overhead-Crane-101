In-house overhead crane operator training

Instructor-led corporate program for a manufacturing plant. The instructor presents; operators then take the 100% knowledge gate. This is not a self-paced self-enroll course. Tracking, designation, and practical sign-off live in the company’s system.

Plant default. 29 CFR 1910.179 · ANSI B30.2.0-1967 as incorporated at §1910.179(b)(2) · §1910.184 slings · §1910.147 LOTO · designated personnel §1910.179(b)(8).
Other customer (in the ACS, not this plant’s default): construction §1926.1438 / §1926.1427, and EM 385-1-1 Ch. 16. Canada is an overlay, not a fork.

Run a session

  1. Open the instructor script for the module in order (OHC-01 first).
  2. Present the gated HTML. Practice questions are teaching checks.
  3. Then run the Final Knowledge Check — that is the corporate knowledge test (100%).
  4. Do not skip ahead. The instructor releases the next module.
