# 📕 OHC — §1910.179 Citation Pack (verified)

Every claim below was read **verbatim from the archived §1910.179 text**, paragraph by paragraph, on 2026-08-07. eCFR was unreachable, so this was built from the archive — per the register's own rule. ⬅ [[_Crane Standards — Index]] · [[🗺️ Home]]

> [!important] What this is for
> The OHC ACS build spec cites §1910.179 broadly but pins almost nothing at paragraph level. This pack supplies the sentence-level anchors, and flags the places where the spec attributes to EM 385 something that is actually OSHA — which matters, because **the two branches behave differently.**

> [!warning] Branch rule — read this before citing anything below
> **Facility branch** (permanently installed, §1926.1438(a)): all of §1910.179 applies except (b)(1).
> **Construction branch** (not permanently installed, §1926.1438(b)): only the enumerated paragraphs apply — **(b)(5),(6),(7) · (e)(1),(3),(5),(6) · (f)(1),(4) · (g) · (h)(1),(3) · (k) · (n) · (a) definitions except *hoist* and *load***.
> Anything below marked 🔴 **falls away on the construction branch.** Teaching it as universal is a defect.

## ⭐ The one that was missing — the Designation Gate's own citation

> **§1910.179(b)(8) Designated personnel** — *"Only designated personnel shall be permitted to operate a crane covered by this section."*

🔴 **Not in the §1926.1438(b)(2) list.** That is precisely why the construction branch reaches for §1926.1427 certification instead — and it is what makes the two-gate architecture legally legible.

**Apply →** `OHC.01.C.K2` and `OHC.12.C.K1` currently assert the Designation Gate with no citation. This is the citation. Pair it with **§1910.179(l)(3)(i)** — *"Adjustments and repairs shall be done only by designated personnel"* — which shows the same term scoping a second role.

Note also the standard's other actor: **"appointed person"** (used at (j)(4), (k)(2), (m)(1), (m)(2), (n)(4)(i)). The ACS uses neither term. It should.

## 🔁 Four attributions to correct

The spec sources these to EM 385 16-8.aa. They are OSHA text, and two of them are branch-dependent.

| Spec element | Spec says | Actually | Branch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `OHC.02.C.K4` · `OHC.08.A.K3` warning device | EM 385 16-8.aa(4) | **§1910.179(i)** — *"Except for floor-operated cranes a gong or other effective warning signal shall be provided for each crane equipped with a power traveling mechanism."* | 🔴 facility only |
| `OHC.04.A.K1` rated load marking | EM 385 16-8.aa(3) | **§1910.179(b)(5)** — marked on each side; each hoist or load block marked where multiple units; *"clearly legible from the ground or floor."* | ✅ both |
| `OHC.07.B.K4` · `OHC.08.B.K1` clearances | EM 385 16-8.aa(5) | **§1910.179(b)(6)(i)** — *minimum 3 in overhead, 2 in laterally*, per **CMAA Specification No. 61** (incorporated by reference); **(b)(6)(ii)** walkway obstruction; **(b)(7)** clearance between parallel cranes | ✅ both |
| `OHC.08.C.K2` outdoor securing | EM 385 16-8.aa(7) | **§1910.179(b)(4)** — outdoor storage bridges shall have **automatic rail clamps** and a **wind-indicating device** alarming the operator at a predetermined wind velocity | 🔴 facility only |

> [!note] CMAA Spec No. 61
> (b)(6)(i) incorporates **CMAA Specification No. 61** by reference (formerly Electric Overhead Crane Institute). The OHC spec names CMAA 70 and 74 only. 61 is the one that is actually law.

**And the operating counterpart to the warning device**, which the spec misses entirely:

> **§1910.179(n)(3)(xi)** — *"When starting the bridge and when the load or hook approaches near or over personnel, the warning signal shall be sounded."* ✅ both branches

(i) is the *equipment* requirement; (n)(3)(xi) is the *operator's duty*. Different elements, different branches.

## 🎯 §1910.179(n) — Handling the load ✅ applies on BOTH branches

The densest operator-conduct paragraph in the standard, and the spec cites almost none of it.

**Attaching — (n)(2)**
- (i) hoist chain/rope free from kinks or twists, **not wrapped around the load**
- (ii) load attached to the load block hook by slings **or other approved devices**
- (iii) care taken that the sling clears all obstacles

**Moving — (n)(3)**
- (i) *"The load shall be well secured and properly balanced in the sling or lifting device before it is lifted more than a few inches."* → the regulatory basis for `OHC.06.C.K2` test-lift technique
- (ii) before hoisting: rope not kinked · multiple part lines not twisted around each other · **hook brought over the load in such a manner as to prevent swinging**
- (iii) during hoisting: no sudden acceleration or deceleration · load does not contact obstructions
- **(iv) SIDE PULLS — correction needed.** *"Cranes shall not be used for side pulls except when specifically authorized by a responsible person who has determined that the stability of the crane is not thereby endangered and that various parts of the crane will not be overstressed."* → `OHC.07.A.K3` states a flat prohibition. **It is conditional, not absolute.** Teaching it as absolute is wrong and will be contradicted by any experienced operator in the room.
- (v) while any employee is on the load or hook: **no hoisting, lowering, or traveling** → `OHC.08.A.K2`
- (vi) employer shall require the operator **avoid carrying loads over people** → `OHC.08.A.K1`
- **(vii) BRAKE TEST — uncited and specific.** *"The operator shall test the brakes each time a load approaching the rated load is handled. The brakes shall be tested by raising the load a few inches and applying the brakes."* → `OHC.07.A.K2`, `OHC.07.A.S2`, `OHC.02.B.S2`
- **(viii) TWO FULL WRAPS — absent from the ACS entirely.** *"The load shall not be lowered below the point where less than two full wraps of rope remain on the hoisting drum."* → **needs a new K element in OHC-07 Task A.**
- **(ix) MULTIPLE-CRANE LIFTS.** *"When two or more cranes are used to lift a load one qualified responsible person shall be in charge of the operation. He shall analyze the operation and instruct all personnel involved in the proper positioning, rigging of the load, and the movements to be made."* → `OHC.04.C.R3` frames this as "engineered load share." The regulation's answer is **one responsible person in charge.**
- (x) employer shall insure the operator **does not leave the controls while the load is suspended** → `OHC.08.A.K5`

**Hoist limit switch — (n)(4)** ⭐ *the single richest sub-paragraph for OHC-05*
- **(i)** *"At the beginning of each operator's shift, the upper limit switch of each hoist shall be tried out under no load. Extreme care shall be exercised; the block shall be 'inched' into the limit or run in at slow speed. If the switch does not operate properly, the appointed person shall be immediately notified."*
  → `OHC.05.A.K3` / `OHC.05.A.S2` / `OHC.02.B.S1`. The spec has the technique right but omits **shift-start frequency** and the **duty to notify the appointed person.**
- **(ii)** *"The hoist limit switch which controls the upper limit of travel of the load block shall never be used as an operating control."*
  → `OHC.02.B.K4` and `OHC.07.A.R3` assert exactly this with no citation. This is it, verbatim.

## 🔍 §1910.179(j) — Inspection 🔴 facility branch only

**Not in the §1926.1438(b)(2) list.** On the construction branch, inspection comes from **§1926.1412** and wire rope from **§1926.1413** — neither of which appears anywhere in the OHC ACS. That is a live gap in OHC-05 and OHC-06.

**Intervals — (j)(1)(ii)**
- **Frequent = daily to monthly**
- **Periodic = 1 to 12 month intervals**
→ `OHC.05.A` and `OHC.05.B` describe both regimes without ever stating the intervals. These are the numbers.

**Frequent scope — (j)(2)**
| Item | Frequency |
| --- | --- |
| All functional operating mechanisms for maladjustment | **Daily** |
| Deterioration/leakage in air or hydraulic systems | **Daily** |
| **Hooks** — deformation or cracks | Visual **daily**; **monthly with a certification record** |
| **Hoist chains** — wear, twist, distorted links, stretch | Visual **daily**; **monthly with a certification record** |
| Functional mechanisms for excessive wear | (interval per (j)(1)(ii)) |
| Rope reeving vs manufacturer's recommendations | (interval per (j)(1)(ii)) |

> [!important] The certification record — three required fields
> For hooks (j)(2)(iii), chains (j)(2)(iv), and ropes (m)(1)/(m)(2), the record must include **the date of inspection, the signature of the person who performed it, and the serial number or other identifier of the item inspected.** → `OHC.05.A.K5`, `OHC.12.B.K2`. The ACS says "documentation per facility program"; the standard specifies the fields.

**Hook removal criteria — (j)(2)(iii)**
Cracks · **more than 15% in excess of normal throat opening** · **more than 10° twist** from the plane of the unbent hook.
> ⚠️ This is the OSHA figure. **ASME B30.10 is stricter** (5%, not to exceed ¼ in, plus any visible bend or twist). The DOE Hanford manual Ch. 5 resolves the conflict under a heading called *Inconsistent Standards*: **follow ASME on the criteria, follow OSHA on the records** (OSHA requires monthly documented hook inspection; B30.10 does not). Teach both figures and the rule for choosing. → `OHC.05.A.K4`, `OHC.05.C.K2`

**Periodic scope — (j)(3)** — deformed/cracked/corroded members · loose bolts or rivets · cracked or worn sheaves and drums · worn/cracked/distorted pins, bearings, shafts, gears, rollers, locking and clamping devices · excessive wear on brake parts, linings, pawls, ratchets · load/wind/other indicators over full range · powerplants · chain drive sprockets and chain stretch · **electrical apparatus for pitting or deterioration of controller contactors, limit switches and pushbutton stations**
→ `OHC.05.B.K1` says "structural, mechanical, and electrical deep scope." This is the actual list.

**Idle and standby — (j)(4)** ⭐ closes `OHC.05.B.K4`, which currently says only "concepts"
| Condition | Requirement before return to service |
| --- | --- |
| Idle **1–6 months** | (j)(2) frequent + (m)(2) rope |
| Idle **over 6 months** | (j)(2) + (j)(3) complete + (m)(2) rope |
| **Standby** cranes | (j)(2) + (m)(2), **at least semi-annually** |

## 🧵 §1910.179(m) — Rope inspection 🔴 facility branch only

**(m)(1) Running ropes** — *"a thorough inspection of all ropes shall be made at least once a month"* with a **certification record** (date · signature · rope identifier) kept on file where readily available to appointed personnel.

The six conditions the standard names as causing appreciable loss of strength:
1. Reduction of rope diameter below nominal — loss of core support, internal or external corrosion, or wear of outside wires
2. Number of broken outside wires **and the degree of distribution or concentration** of them
3. Worn outside wires
4. Corroded or broken wires **at end connections**
5. Corroded, cracked, bent, worn, or **improperly applied end connections**
6. Severe kinking, crushing, cutting, or unstranding

→ `OHC.05.C.K2` lists "broken wires, kinking, crushing, corrosion, stretch, and gauge wear." Close, but it misses **end connections entirely** (two of the six conditions) and misses **distribution/concentration** of broken wires, which is the part that separates a competent read from a wire count.

**(m)(2) Idle ropes** — rope idle ≥1 month gets a thorough inspection **before use, by an appointed person whose approval is required**, with a certification record. → `OHC.05.B.K4`, `OHC.05.B.S1`

## 🔧 §1910.179(l) — Maintenance 🔴 facility branch only

**(l)(1)** *"A preventive maintenance program based on the crane manufacturer's recommendations shall be established."*

**(l)(2)(i) — the lockout sequence before adjustments or repairs.** Five steps, and the ACS captures none of them at this level:
1. Run the crane to a location causing **least interference** with other cranes and operations
2. All controllers at the **off** position
3. Main or emergency switch **open and locked open**
4. Warning or **"out of order" signs** on the crane, **and on the floor beneath or on the hook where visible from the floor**
5. Where other cranes share the runway, **rail stops or other suitable means** to prevent interference with the idle crane

→ `OHC.05.C.K3` (tag-out and control of a defective crane) and `OHC.08.B` multi-crane. Step 5 is the one operators forget and it is the one that gets a mechanic hit.

**(l)(2)(ii)** — after repairs, crane not operated until **all guards reinstalled, safety devices reactivated, maintenance equipment removed**. → `OHC.05.B.K5`, `OHC.05.B.S3`

**(l)(3)(i)** — unsafe conditions from (j) **corrected before operation resumes**; adjustments and repairs **only by designated personnel**. → `OHC.05.C.K1`, `OHC.05.C.K4`

**(l)(3)(iii)(a)** — hooks with (j)(2)(iii) defects **shall be discarded**. *"Repairs by welding or reshaping are not generally recommended."* If attempted, only under competent supervision, and the hook must be **load tested per (k)(2)** before further use. → `OHC.05.C.K4`

## 🧪 §1910.179(k) — Testing ✅ applies on BOTH branches

- **(k)(1)(i)** before initial use, all new and altered cranes tested for: hoisting and lowering · trolley travel · bridge travel · **limit switches, locking and safety devices**
- **(k)(1)(ii)** hoist limit switch trip setting determined **by tests with an empty hook at increasing speeds up to maximum**, positioned to trip in time to prevent hook or block contact with any part of the trolley → this is the regulatory reason the limit switch is a *test device, not a control*
- **(k)(2)** rated load test **not more than 125% of rated load** unless the manufacturer recommends otherwise; **test reports on file where readily available to appointed personnel** → `OHC.04.A.K3` (rated vs test load), `OHC.12.B.K3`

## 🏗️ Structural, cab and access — mostly facility branch

**(e) Stops, bumpers, rail sweeps, guards** — (e)(1),(3),(5),(6) apply on both branches; **(e)(2) bridge bumpers and (e)(4) rail sweeps do not.**
- (e)(1) trolley stops at limits of travel; fastened to resist forces applied when contacted; a stop engaging the wheel tread at least equal to the wheel radius
- (e)(3) trolley bumpers — deceleration **not to exceed 4.7 ft/s²** at one-third rated load speed; each trolley on a shared bridge bumpered on adjacent ends
- 🔴 (e)(2) bridge bumpers — deceleration **not to exceed 3 ft/s²** at 20% rated load speed; energy capacity to stop at **≥40%** of rated load speed; mounted so there is **no direct shear on bolts**
- 🔴 (e)(4) rail sweeps — bridge trucks equipped with sweeps extending below the top of the rail, ahead of the truck wheels
- (e)(5) guards for hoisting ropes where fouling or chafing is possible, **and to prevent contact between bridge conductors and hoisting ropes**
- (e)(6) guards for exposed moving parts; securely fastened; **each capable of supporting a 200-lb person without permanent distortion** unless positioned where a person cannot step on it

→ `OHC.02.A.K3` names stops, bumpers and sweeps. These are the criteria behind them, and the branch split is teachable content in its own right.

**(f)(1) Brakes** ✅ both branches
- (i) each independent hoisting unit shall have **at least one self-setting holding brake** applied to the motor shaft or gear train
- (ii) each unit — **except worm-geared hoists whose worm angle prevents the load accelerating in lowering** — shall additionally have **control braking means to prevent overspeeding**
→ `OHC.02.B.K2` (hoist brakes and control braking means). The worm-gear exception is the detail that explains *why* two braking systems exist.

**(c) Cabs** 🔴 facility only — arrangement giving **a full view of the load hook in all positions** · **minimum 3 in clearance** from fixed structures in the cab's area of possible movement · access by fixed ladder, stairs or platform **requiring no step over any gap exceeding 12 in** · **carbon tetrachloride extinguishers shall not be used** · lighting sufficient to see clearly
→ `OHC.03.C.K1`, `OHC.03.C.K3`, `OHC.03.C.K5`

**(d) Footwalks and ladders** 🔴 facility only — footwalk on the drive side along the entire bridge on top-running cab-operated cranes where headroom allows · **never less than 48 in headroom** · rigid construction, **≥50 lb/ft² distributed load**, antislip surface · gantry cranes shall have ladders or stairways **from the ground to the footwalk or cab platform**
→ `OHC.02.A.K4`

**(g) Electric equipment** ✅ both branches — wiring per Subpart S · **control circuit voltage ≤600 V** a.c. or d.c. · **voltage at pendant push-buttons ≤150 V a.c. / 300 V d.c.**
→ `OHC.02.C.K3`, `OHC.03.A.K1`

**(h)(1) Sheaves** ✅ both branches — grooves smooth and free from defects that could damage rope · guards on sheaves carrying momentarily-unloaded ropes · **close-fitting guards on bottom-block sheaves to prevent fouling when the block is on the ground with ropes loose** · pockets and flanges dimensioned so hoist chain does not catch or bind · all running sheaves lubricated (sealed/shielded bearings satisfy this)
→ `OHC.02.B.K1`, `OHC.02.B.S3`

**(h)(2)(i) Ropes** — manufacturer's recommendation followed; **rated load ÷ number of parts of rope shall not exceed 20% of nominal breaking strength** (i.e. a 5:1 design factor). → `OHC.04.A.K3`

**(o) Other requirements** 🔴 facility only — **hands free while using ladders**; oversized articles raised and lowered **by hand line** · cab housekeeping: clothing and belongings stored so as not to interfere with access or operation; tools, oil cans, waste, fuses **in the tool box, not loose in the cab** · **operators familiar with the operation and care of the fire extinguishers provided**
→ `OHC.03.C.K5`, `OHC.11.C.K3`

**(b)(3) Modifications** 🔴 facility only — cranes may be modified and rerated provided the modifications **and the supporting structure** are checked for the new rated load **by a qualified engineer or the equipment manufacturer**; tested per (k)(2); **new rated load displayed per (b)(5)**.
→ `OHC.05.B.K5` (modified/repaired/altered crane verification) — currently uncited, and this is a clean anchor.

## 📌 Net changes this pack implies for the ACS

**New elements needed**
- OHC-07 Task A — **two full wraps of rope on the drum** at lowest point of travel, (n)(3)(viii)
- OHC-07 Task A / OHC-04 Task C — **one qualified responsible person in charge** of any two-crane lift, (n)(3)(ix)
- OHC-05 Task A — **shift-start** limit switch test + **notify the appointed person** on failure, (n)(4)(i)
- OHC-01 Task C — **designated personnel** (b)(8) and **appointed person** as distinct standard-defined roles

**Corrections**
- `OHC.07.A.K3` — side pulls are **conditionally permitted with responsible-person authorization**, not flatly prohibited
- `OHC.05.C.K2` — rope criteria must include **end connections** and **distribution/concentration** of broken wires
- Four EM 385 attributions → §1910.179, two of them branch-dependent (table above)

**Additions to references**
- **§1926.1412** (inspection) and **§1926.1413** (wire rope) for the construction branch — absent from all 12 modules
- **CMAA Specification No. 61** — incorporated by reference at (b)(6)(i)
